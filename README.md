# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Introduction
My favorite series is **Stranger Things**, a science fiction and horror series created by the Duffer Brothers.

The story takes place in a small town called Hawkins, where a group of friends discovers mysterious events and supernatural creatures.

- It combines mystery, adventure, and science fiction.
